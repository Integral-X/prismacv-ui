# Cookie Preferences

PrismaCV uses cookies to keep your session active, improve reliability, and understand product usage.

## Cookie categories

### Required cookies

These cookies are necessary for authentication, security, and core product behavior. They cannot be disabled while using PrismaCV.

### Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand aggregate usage patterns so we can improve product quality and performance.

### Product improvement cookies

These cookies support experiments and feature quality measurements. They are optional.

## How to manage cookies

- Use browser controls to clear or block cookies.
- You can revisit this page any time for the latest policy guidance.
- Blocking required cookies may prevent sign-in or core functionality.
