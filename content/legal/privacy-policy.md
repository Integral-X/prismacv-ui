# Privacy Policy

PrismaCV helps job seekers build resumes, cover letters, and career workflows. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

## Information we collect

- Account information such as name, email address, and encrypted authentication credentials.
- Resume and profile content you create, import, or edit in the product.
- Billing metadata provided by Stripe (for example subscription plan status and customer identifiers).
- Usage logs and diagnostics used to keep the service secure and reliable.

## How we use your information

- To provide and improve PrismaCV features.
- To authenticate users and protect account access.
- To process subscription billing and manage plan entitlements.
- To investigate incidents, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

## Data retention

We retain account and resume data while your account is active. You can request deletion by contacting support. We may retain limited records when required for fraud prevention, finance, or legal compliance.

## Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to run PrismaCV, such as hosting, analytics, and payment processors. These providers process data under contractual obligations.

## Your controls

- You can update profile and resume content at any time.
- You can request account deletion and data export from support.
- You can manage optional cookies on the Cookie Preferences page.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact [support@prismacv.com](mailto:support@prismacv.com).
